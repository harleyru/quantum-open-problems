# Persistent-Advantage Search Log

## Scope

The search covered the local Mothe-Branciard-Abbott amplitude-damping
examples, WLP's SQL and HL examples, the local parallel SDP for N up to 4,
the symmetric parallel probes through N=64, the QC-QC scalar recurrence, and
the cited literature on adaptive, causal-superposition, and QC-QC strategies.

## Findings

- The Mothe example retains a finite-use QC-QC advantage at three uses.
- The tested symmetric parallel probes exceed the rate of independently
  repeating that reported three-use block, but this does not optimize all
  QC-QC blocks.
- No tested family produced evidence of a persistent non-unit asymptotic
  ratio or a larger scaling exponent.
- WLP's source theorem, conditional on its regularity and attainability
  hypotheses, rules out a leading-order advantage for the stated repeated
  finite-dimensional model.

## Correct interpretation

The numerical search is not exhaustive and "no example found" is not a
counterexample or a universal proof. The universal no-leading-order claim is
therefore attributed to WLP's theorem and its audited assumptions, while the
local computations are reported only as consistency evidence. Finite-query
and subleading advantages remain compatible with all observations.
